How to Use Our Website

A comprehensive guide to navigate and utilize all features of our tourism research platform

Registering an Account

To access full features of our website, you need to create an account. Follow these steps:

  1. Click on the "Register" button in the header.
  2. Fill in your personal details including name, email, and password.
  3. Agree to the terms and conditions.
  4. Click "Submit" to create your account.
  5. Check your email for a verification link and click it to activate your account.

Once registered, you can log in anytime using your email and password.

Tip: Use a strong password with a mix of letters, numbers, and symbols for better security.

Uploading Documents

Our platform allows you to upload research documents and reports. Here's how:

  1. Log in to your account.
  2. Navigate to the "Upload" page from the menu.
  3. Click "Choose File" to select your document (supported formats: PDF, DOC, DOCX).
  4. Add a title, description, and relevant tags for your document.
  5. Select the appropriate category (e.g., Research, Policy, Reports).
  6. Click "Upload" to submit your document.

Your uploaded documents will be reviewed by our team before being published in the library.

  • Maximum file size: 10MB
  • Ensure documents are original and properly cited
  • Contact support if you encounter any upload issues

Using the Library

Our library contains a comprehensive collection of tourism-related research and documents. To make the most of it:

  • Search: Use the search bar to find specific topics or keywords.
  • Browse Categories: Explore documents by categories like Research Papers, Policy Documents, or Reports.
  • Download: Click on any document to view details and download if available.
  • Save Favorites: Log in to bookmark documents for easy access later.
  • Share: Use the share buttons to distribute links to important documents.

The library is regularly updated with new content. Check back often for the latest research and publications.
